# Organization Principles

## Delivery

- Ship in thin vertical slices.
- Prefer reversible changes.
- Make progress visible through artifacts and board state.

## Review

- Ask for human approval when the decision changes scope, risk, or rollout behavior.
- Prefer concrete feedback over vague criticism.

## Knowledge

- Capture durable decisions once.
- Reuse org guidance before inventing repo-local policy.
- Promote project lessons to org principles only when they generalize.
